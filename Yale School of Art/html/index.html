School Overview

Yale School of Art, a graduate school dedicated to the exploration of art in all its forms — since 1869.

Scroll

The Yale School of Art opened its doors on October 15, 1869, becoming the first art school affiliated with a university of higher learning in the United States.

Today, the Yale School of Art is a graduate school that confers Master of Fine Arts degrees in Graphic Design, Painting/Printmaking, Photography, and Sculpture. Artists and designers of significant promise and strong motivation are provided an educational context in which they can explore the potential of their own talents.

Programs & Community

MFA Programs & Academic Environment

The school offers rigorous MFA programs in Graphic Design, Painting/Printmaking, Photography, and Sculpture, fostering a community of critical dialogue and creative exploration.

The rich academic environment of Yale along with rigorous, structured coursework rounds out an educational experience designed to prepare students for a lifetime of independent work.

The students' primary educational experience at Yale is centered on their own studio activity as supported by the experience, knowledge, and skills gained from rigorous, structured courses.

Student Experience & Community

The graduate student experience is centered on each artist's own studio practice, supported by faculty, visiting artists and critics, and perhaps most importantly, by one another, as they develop a personal studio practice.

Yale School of Art values and generates intense critical dialogue through interactions with peers, distinguished visitors, and by a faculty composed of professional artists of national and international renown.

Students are routinely exposed to varying aesthetic positions through encounters with faculty members and visitors.

Creative Development

The School is devoted not only to the refinement of visual skills, but also to the cultivation of the mind.

Students must bring creative force and imagination to their own development, for these qualities cannot be taught—they can only be stimulated.

MFA Degrees

The degree of the Master of Fine Arts is only offered by the School of Art, where students remain in residence for a minimum of two years, during which performance is reviewed and academic standing is renewed annually.

The degree is conferred by the University upon recommendation of faculty following the completion of all coursework and a thesis presentation.

Participation in the MFA program at the Yale School of Art indicates a level of seriousness and commitment to artistic excellence and scholarship; the conferring of the degree implies confidence in the students' ability to maintain dedication to their craft.
