    Faculty Directory

    The following directory reflects the faculty and staff at the Yale School of Art during the 2026–2027 academic year.

    Faculty Governing Board

    Tenured and tenure-track faculty members together with the Stavros Niarchos Foundation Dean at the Yale School of Art constitute its Faculty Governing Board. The Faculty Governing Board is entrusted with matters relating to the academic policy and government of the school.

    Academic Leadership

    Tenured and tenure-track faculty members together with the Dean constitute the Faculty Governing Board.

    Faculty appointments in each area of graduate study correspond to varying levels of engagement at the School of Art. This page reflects the faculty and staff at the Yale School of Art during the 2026–2027 academic year.

    Full-time Faculty: On campus regularly each week for courses, critiques, studio visits, advising, and other service. Full-time faculty also includes the Directors and Assistant Directors for each area of study.

    Part-time Faculty: Teaching or co-teaching a weekly course or courses.

    Visiting Critics: On campus occasionally for studio/desk visits or workshops.
