    Text Size

    Adjust the base reading size across the site (WCAG 1.4.4 Resize Text).

    Increases border, focus indicator, and text contrast for enhanced readability (WCAG 1.4.6 / 1.4.11).

    Minimizes animations, hover lifts, and smooth-scroll transitions throughout the interface (WCAG 2.3.3).
