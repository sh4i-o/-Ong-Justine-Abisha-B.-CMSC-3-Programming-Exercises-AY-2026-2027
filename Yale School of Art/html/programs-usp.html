    Undergraduate & Summer Programs

    Yale School of Art, a graduate school dedicated to the exploration of art in all its forms — since 1869.

    Scroll

    Yale College, the undergraduate division of Yale University, offers a Bachelor of Arts degree program with a major in art.

    Students may concentrate in a medium such as Painting & Printmaking, sculpture, graphic design, photography, or filmmaking. Suggested program guidelines and specific requirements for the various areas of concentration are available from the director of undergraduate studies (DUS) and departmental faculty. Undergraduate applicants wishing to major in art at Yale must apply to Yale College directly.

    Students in this area will develop an understanding of the visual arts through a studio-based curriculum, apply fundamentals of art across a variety of media and disciplines, relate the practice of making art to the fields of art history and theory, and gain a high level of proficiency in at least one artistic discipline. For more information about the program including prerequisites, courses, and reviews, please visit the Bulletin of Yale University.

    Summer Programs & Facilities

    The Yale Norfolk School of Art

    The Yale Norfolk School of Art is a six-week undergraduate summer intensive residency program for 26 rising seniors. The school is located on the picturesque Ellen Battell Stoeckel Estate in Norfolk, Connecticut and is supported by the Ellen Battell Stoeckel Trust. The Trust generously subsidizes much of the cost of attendance.

    Yale Summer Session

    Yale Summer Session provides academically rigorous and innovative undergraduate programs and courses to Yale and visiting students across partners and around the world. Students come to Yale Summer Session to take Yale College courses in New Haven, online, or abroad, or to hone their craft at the Yale Writers’ Workshop or in the Summer Drama Program.

    The Artist Collective Summer School

    The Artist Collective Summer School is designed to be an informal, flexible, and lively program of workshops and events in which the participating students and instructors will have a leading role. It is intended to provide the opportunity for an in-depth immersion in the work of artists from each area, and for Yale-based students to spend time with their UK-based peers and with other members of the UK’s art community.
