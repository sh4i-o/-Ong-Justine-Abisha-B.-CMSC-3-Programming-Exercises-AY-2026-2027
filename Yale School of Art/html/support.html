    Support

    Support the School

    Gifts of all sizes directly sustain Yale School of Art students, expanding scholarship support, studio resources, and artistic inquiry.

    Scroll
    Annual Fund

    Whether made by alumni, parents, friends, or anyone interested in art, gifts to Yale School of Art’s annual fund directly support its students. Gifts of all sizes make an impact, with priority given to scholarship funds.

    Make a Gift ↗
    Ways to Give
    Long-Term Investment

    Endowment Funds

    Patrons can provide ongoing support to the School of Art by making gift commitments of $50,000 or more to the Yale Endowment for a period of up to five years.

    Endowment gifts are an investment in the future of the School and may support scholarships, faculty, research and travel, and capital needs. These gifts provide distributed income to the School each year through investment.

    Legacy & Estate Support

    Planned Giving

    Patrons may support the School through bequests, annuities, trusts, and other financial vehicles.

    If possible, please contact the Director of Development at the Yale School of Art to discuss your planned gift to ensure that your intentions are carried out as you envision.

    To discuss making an endowment or planned gift, contact:
