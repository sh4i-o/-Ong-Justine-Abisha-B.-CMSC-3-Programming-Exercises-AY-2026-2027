    Tours

    The School of Art does not offer tours of its facilities, nor does it accommodate walk-in tours.

    Tours of the broader Yale campus are run by the undergraduate admission office. Yale College students lead the tours, providing a glimpse into the history and architecture of the University. Refer to the Yale College Admissions website for more information, including tour schedules.

    Undergraduate Student Art Majors may arrange student-led tours of the undergraduate art school by contacting the office of the Director of Undergraduate Studies in Art at art.dus@yale.edu or 203-432-2600. Tours are typically offered during regular business hours of the school year (Monday–Friday, 8 am to 4 pm; September through May). Requests will be accommodated when possible with ample notice.

    Public Events & Open Studios

    The Yale School of Art hosts a regular series of public events during term time, including panels, artist lectures, and exhibition receptions. Find upcoming public events on our calendar.

    Every April, the School of Art opens its doors for Open Studios weekend, with work presented from the departments of Graphic Design, Painting and Printmaking, Photography, and Sculpture. In 2027, Open Studios weekend is Saturday, April 10 and Sunday, April 11.
