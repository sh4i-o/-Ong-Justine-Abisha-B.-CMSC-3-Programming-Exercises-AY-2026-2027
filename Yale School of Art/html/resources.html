    Resources

    Yale School of Art offers a broad array of resources that may be accessed by Undergraduate and Graduate students, as well as Alumni.

    Scroll

    Yale School of Art offers a broad array of resources that may be accessed by Undergraduate and Graduate students, as well as Alumni. These resources, which include facilities, are detailed here in support of the entire Yale School of Art community.

    Graduate Student Resources

    Yale School of Art is committed to fostering a safe, just, and equitable environment for learning, experimentation and discovery while encouraging healthy habits in its students. Questions, concerns, or feedback regarding student relations and academic affairs should be directed to Taryn Wolf, Director of Academic Administration and Dean’s Designee. They may also contact the Director of Graduate Study (DGS) or Graduate Coordinator in their area.

    Academic Calendar & Student Hub

      • Access the Yale School of Art Academic Calendar, course registration portals (Yale Course Search), academic regulations, degree progress requirements, and University libraries.
      • View School Calendar · Official Graduate Portal
      • Information on MFA tuition scholarships, federal and institutional loans, student employment opportunities, teaching assistantships, and emergency financial support.
      • Admissions & Aid Overview · Yale Financial Aid
      • Student-led organizations, cross-departmental critiques, School of Art cultural centers, and community programming across 1156 Chapel Street and 32 Edgewood Avenue.
      • Yale Belonging & Cultural Centers
      • Comprehensive medical coverage through Yale Health, Mental Health & Counseling services, studio Environmental Health & Safety (EHS) protocols, and campus shuttle/security resources.
      • Yale Health · Environmental Health & Safety
      • Confidential support, reporting options, and prevention resources through the SHARE Center (Sexual Harassment and Assault Response & Education), Title IX Office, and University-Wide Committee on Sexual Misconduct.
      • Yale SHARE Center · Sexual Misconduct Response
      • On-campus graduate dormitories, University-owned apartments, off-campus New Haven housing listings, dining plans, and family support resources.
      • Yale Housing
      • Annual notification of student rights under FERPA regarding access to education records, directory information privacy settings, and consent for disclosure.
      • Yale Registrar FERPA Guidelines
      • Official Yale University policies governing equal opportunity, nondiscrimination, academic integrity, accessibility accommodations (Student Accessibility Services), and free expression.
      • Student Accessibility Services
    Undergraduate Resources

    Resources for Current Art Majors

      • Guidance on studio course material fees, financial aid adjustments for required art supplies, and departmental material stipends for declared Yale College Art majors.
      • Undergraduate Program Details
      • Digital and analog camera equipment checkout procedures for students enrolled in introductory photography and filmmaking courses through the School of Art Media Checkout cage.
      • Explore School Facilities
      • Shared painting, printmaking, ceramics, and darkroom workspaces located within Yale’s 14 residential colleges, available to undergraduates outside of scheduled class hours.
      • Access and safety orientation requirements for the School of Art wood and metal shops, Yale Center for Engineering Innovation and Design (CEID), and Digital Media Center for the Arts (DMCA).
      • View Fabrication Shops
      • Campus galleries and residential college exhibition venues open for undergraduate solo and group proposals throughout the academic year.
      • Curricular exhibition scheduling for the Undergraduate Senior Exhibition and open student shows at Green Hall Gallery (1156 Chapel St), 32 Edgewood Gallery, and 63 Audubon.
      • Exhibitions Overview
      • Peer advising network pairing prospective and newly declared Yale College Art majors with senior Art majors and MFA graduate mentors.

    Student Grants and Fellowships

      • Small project grants administered by Heads of College and Residential College Deans to support creative productions, exhibitions, and senior thesis materials.
      • Yale College Safety Net funding for students experiencing unexpected financial hardship who need assistance purchasing essential studio materials or course equipment.
      • CPA Awards supporting student-led visual arts exhibitions, publications, installations, and interdisciplinary performances on campus.
      • Competitive summer stipends supporting independent studio research, archival study, and international artistic residencies between academic years.
      • Funding for unpaid or low-paid summer internships and studio apprenticeships with established artists, museums, design studios, and non-profit arts organizations.
      • Awarded annually to a Yale College student whose artistic work demonstrates exceptional originality, commitment, and creative exploration.
      • Supports domestic or international travel for visual arts research and independent studio practice.
      • Provides support for graduating seniors and recent graduates pursuing independent artistic study and creative projects outside the United States.
      • Fellowship awarded to promising undergraduate art students to advance their studio work and artistic development.
      • Endowed program supporting undergraduate research and creative practice in the arts across Yale College.
      • Annual prizes recognizing excellence in undergraduate typography, book design, poster design, and letterpress or digital printing.

    External Residencies and Awards

      • Curated directory of summer and semester-long artist residencies—including Yale Norfolk, Skowhegan, Ox-Bow, and Anderson Ranch—open to undergraduate applicants.
      • External grant databases, open calls, and residency directories including Alliance of Artists Communities, NYFA Source, and College Art Association opportunities.

    Post-Grad Fellowships

      • A post-baccalaureate studio residency providing recent graduates with dedicated studio space, stipend support, and exhibition opportunities in Hartford, Connecticut.
    Alumni Resources

    Alumni of the School of Art have access to general Yale University alumni resources. Some of those resources are listed below. A complete list can be found at alumni.yale.edu.

    Resources

      • Set up or manage a lifelong @aya.yale.edu alumni email forwarding address through the Yale Alumni Association.
      • Yale Alumni Association
      • Request official electronic or paper academic transcripts and replacement MFA/BA diplomas through the Yale University Registrar’s Office.
      • Yale Registrar Transcripts
      • Degree and enrollment verification services for alumni, employers, and academic institutions via the National Student Clearinghouse and University Registrar.
      • Enrollment & Degree Verifications
      • Alumni borrowing privileges at Haas Arts Library and Sterling Memorial Library, plus remote access to JSTOR, Project MUSE, and selected online scholarly databases.
      • Yale Library Alumni Services
      • Connect with fellow Yale School of Art graduates and mentor current students through Cross Campus, Yale’s online networking and mentorship platform.
      • Join Cross Campus
